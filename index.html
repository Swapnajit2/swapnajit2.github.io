I am currently a fourth-year Integrated PhD student at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, India, working under Prof. Omprokash Das. I work in birational geometry. I am mainly interested in the Minimal Model Program and its applications to the classification of higher-dimensional varieties, including complex projective varieties and Kähler varieties. I am open to learning about and discussing different areas of birational geometry.
