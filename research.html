Research

My research is in birational geometry, with a focus on the Minimal Model Program and its applications to the classification of higher-dimensional varieties in the complex projective and Kähler settings.

I am open to learning about and discussing different areas of birational geometry.

Research interests

  • Birational geometry
  • The Minimal Model Program
  • Classification of higher-dimensional complex projective and Kähler varieties
