Teaching

Since my school days, I have enjoyed discussing mathematics with my friends, seniors, and juniors. Alongside my research, I hope to become a good lecturer and help students understand and enjoy mathematics.

I am happy to mentor my juniors. Although I have not yet had an opportunity to teach a course officially, I teach undergraduate juniors informally and help them prepare for national-level entrance and qualifying examinations such as JAM, NET, GATE, TIFR GS, and NBHM.
